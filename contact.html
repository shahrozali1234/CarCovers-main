COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about size fitment, weather protection, fabric quality, and delivery across Pakistan.

Will this cover scratch or leave swirl marks on my car's paint? ▼
Zero Swirl Marks Guaranteed: Lined with proprietary FleeceGuard™ spun-cotton velvet. 100% safe for factory clear coats, ceramic coatings, car wax, and PPF wraps. The ultra-soft inner lining floats over your paint surface without any contact friction.
Is the cover 100% waterproof in heavy monsoon rains? ▼
10,000mm Hydrostatic Rating: Our Hydro-Tough™ high-density polyethylene membrane with ultrasonic heat-welded seams keeps your vehicle bone dry during continuous days of heavy monsoon downpours. Tested in Pakistan's most intense rainfall conditions.
How does the cover stay secure during strong windstorms? ▼
Dual Wind-Lock Straps: Features heavy-duty click-buckle straps that fasten beneath the chassis plus front/rear elastic hems with mirror pockets, holding firm in winds up to 70 km/h without any flapping or displacement.
What if the cover does not fit my car properly? ▼
100% Exact Fit Guarantee: If the size does not match your car model accurately, we dispatch a free replacement size or provide a 100% refund within 7 days. Simply WhatsApp us your vehicle model and we'll verify before shipping.
What are the payment methods and delivery timeline? ▼
Cash on Delivery (COD) Nationwide: Delivered in 2–4 business days across all 150+ cities in Pakistan via TCS / Leopard Courier. Orders over Rs. 5,000 ship completely free. JazzCash & EasyPaisa also accepted.
Can I use AutoShield covers on a freshly waxed or ceramic coated car? ▼
Yes, 100% Safe for All Coatings: Our FleeceGuard™ inner lining is specifically tested to be non-abrasive on ceramic coatings, car wax, graphene coatings, PPF (paint protection film), and factory clear coats. It will not disturb or degrade your finish in any way.
