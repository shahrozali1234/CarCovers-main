The Lightest Touch

Our lightest indoor car cover: soft-touch, unlined and tailored to your coupe or sedan's exact make, model and year. Made to order in 16 colours.

Designed for vehicles that rarely leave protected environments or face harsh Pakistani monsoon downpours and blazing summer solar rays. The Toyota Corolla Altis & Grande All-Weather Silver Shield is built on a single philosophy: the only thing that should ever touch your paintwork is silence and cotton velvet.

Constructed from a multi-layer composite textile, it drapes over every body panel the way fabric should — without argument. The paint-safe inner lining prevents swirl marks on ceramic coatings and PPF, while the anti-static treatment actively repels dust particles.

Key Properties:

  • Bespoke custom-cut silhouette pattern engineered for your exact vehicle
  • 100% paint-safe FleeceGuard™ soft spun cotton velvet inner lining
  • 99.5% Solar heat and UV deflection layer preventing clear coat fade
  • 10,000mm hydrostatic head waterproof barrier against monsoon rains
  • Dual click-lock wind straps with reinforced mirror pockets and antenna relief
  • Includes luxury matching storage duffle bag with embroidered seal
